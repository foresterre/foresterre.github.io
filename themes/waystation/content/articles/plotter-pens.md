A pen plotter draws every line with a real pen. There is no fill, only lines close together. That limit shapes everything you draw with it.

## Hatching instead of fill

To make an area darker, you draw more lines. To make it lighter, you draw fewer. The angle of the lines also matters: the eye reads parallel lines as one surface.

- A thin black pen for structure.
- A pale blue pen for glow and water.
- An orange pen for warnings, and for things that are broken.

Three pens are enough for almost anything. A fourth pen mostly adds doubt.
